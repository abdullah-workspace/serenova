A clinic built for patients who want clear answers

Serenova Clinic opened in 2014 with three doctors and one idea: seeing a specialist should not take weeks.

How we care for patients

  • Time to listen

    Every consultation is booked for 30 minutes, long enough to describe what is wrong and ask your questions.

  • Plain explanations

    We explain results and options in simple language, in English or Urdu, and write down the next steps for you.

  • Care that continues

    We follow up after visits and keep your records in one place, so every doctor you see has the full picture.

Our story so far

From a small practice to an eight-department clinic.

  1. 2014

    Serenova opens with three doctors and two consultation rooms.

  2. 2017

    Pediatrics joins, and the on-site laboratory opens.

  3. 2020

    Phone and video consultations begin for follow-up visits.

  4. 2023

    The clinic moves to its current building with eight departments and an imaging suite.

  5. 2026

    The patient portal launches for appointments and results.

What is inside the building

Everything most visits need is on one site, so you do not travel between locations.

  • 24 consultation rooms
  • On-site laboratory
  • X-ray and ultrasound
  • Pharmacy
  • Children's waiting area
  • Lifts and wheelchair access
  • Parking for 80 cars
  • Quiet room for prayer
