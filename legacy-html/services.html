Departments and services

Eight departments in one building. See what each team treats, then book directly.

Cardiology

Chest pain, high blood pressure, irregular heartbeat, heart failure.

Our cardiologists find the cause of heart symptoms and build a long-term plan to protect your heart.

  • Heart health check-ups
  • ECG and echocardiogram
  • Blood pressure management
  • Cholesterol and risk review
Book with cardiology

Orthopedics

Joint pain, fractures, sports injuries, arthritis, back and neck pain.

We treat bones, joints and muscles, and aim to get you moving again with the least invasive option that works.

  • Fracture and injury care
  • Joint pain assessment
  • Physiotherapy referral
  • Surgical consultation
Book with orthopedics

Pediatrics

Fever, infections, growth concerns, allergies, vaccinations.

Gentle care for babies, children and teenagers, with a separate waiting area and doctors who are used to nervous patients.

  • Routine child check-ups
  • Vaccination schedule
  • Growth and development review
  • Illness and allergy care
Book with pediatrics

Neurology

Headaches, migraine, dizziness, nerve pain, numbness, seizures.

We look for the cause of symptoms that affect the brain and nerves, and explain each finding step by step.

  • Headache and migraine clinic
  • Nerve conduction tests
  • EEG recording
  • Long-term condition follow-up
Book with neurology

Dermatology

Acne, eczema, rashes, hair loss, fungal infections, skin allergies.

Care for skin, hair and nails, from a persistent rash to a mole you want checked.

  • Skin and hair assessment
  • Allergy patch testing
  • Mole and lesion checks
  • Minor in-clinic procedures
Book with dermatology

General medicine

Fever, infections, diabetes, thyroid, stomach problems, general check-ups.

Your first stop for everyday illness and ongoing conditions. We refer you to a specialist when you need one.

  • Same-week consultations
  • Diabetes and thyroid care
  • Full health check-ups
  • Referrals to specialists
Book with general medicine

Gynecology

Period problems, pregnancy care, fertility questions, menopause.

Private, respectful care for women at every stage, with female doctors available on request.

  • Antenatal check-ups
  • Ultrasound scans
  • Family planning advice
  • Menopause support
Book with gynecology

ENT

Ear infections, hearing loss, sinus trouble, throat pain, snoring.

Ear, nose and throat specialists who treat both quick problems and long-standing ones.

  • Hearing tests
  • Sinus and allergy care
  • Throat and voice assessment
  • Minor procedures
Book with ENT
