How a visit works

Four steps, and you always know what comes next.

  1. Book

    Choose a doctor and a time online, or call the care desk. We confirm by phone the same day.

  2. Check in

    Arrive ten minutes early with a photo ID. Reception already has your details.

  3. See your doctor

    Consultations are unhurried. If you need a test, the lab and imaging rooms are on site.

  4. Follow up

    Notes and results go to your patient portal, and you can book your next visit before you leave.

Not sure which department you need?

Call our care desk. A nurse will listen and point you to the right doctor.
